Play it here. Then make it yours.

free browser games from the NodeGX project. Each one is a starting point: copy it, open it with your AI coder, and build your own levels, art and rules.

  1. Play it in your browser. No account, no download.
  2. Copy the one-line command on its card. It fetches only that game.
  3. Open the folder with Claude Code or any AI coder. Its CLAUDE.md says how to add a level.
