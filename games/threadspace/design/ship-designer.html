Design notes

Rules of thumb for spin

  • Radius buys comfort. 1 g at 2 rpm needs a 224 m radius. Inside a 68 m hull you get about half a g at 4 rpm.
  • Flat floors tilt. In a ring of flat-floored rooms, the floor at a room's edge leans 180° ÷ rooms-per-ring. 8 rooms: 22°. 24 rooms: 7.5°. Curve or step the floors.
  • Spin in pairs. A single drum is a gyroscope and fights every turn the ship makes. Two drums spinning opposite ways cancel out.
  • Thrust tips the floor aft by atan(drive ÷ spin g). Rooms hung on pivots, like a centrifuge, can swing to stay level.
  • The bearing is the hard part. A seal that holds air between spinning and fixed structure is the weak point. Crew can transfer at the axis, where the drum is barely moving.

Naval lessons

old ships, space twins
1650–1850

Ship of the line. Guns along both sides, so you aimed by turning the whole ship. Twin: fixed broadside torpedo tubes in the hangar walls.

1906

Dreadnought. All big guns in turrets on the centreline, so every gun could fire to either side; later ships stacked turrets to fire over each other. Twin: turrets on the top and bottom centrelines. Space has no up, so both bear on any side.

1912

All or nothing armour. Very thick armour over a citadel (engines, magazines, steering) and almost none elsewhere. The soft parts could be wrecked without losing the ship. Twin: armour the fixed citadel and the sleeve; leave drums light and clear them at action stations.

1942

Carriers end the battleship era. At Coral Sea and Midway the fleets never saw each other; aircraft outranged guns. Twin: fighters and torpedoes are the long arm. Guns are for close range and for shooting down torpedoes.

1914–now

Submarine and torpedo. A torpedo carries its own motor and steers itself. Twin: launch cold with a coil or gas, coast dark, light the motor late so the target has little time to react.

1980–now

Missiles and point defence. Fleets win by killing incoming missiles with automatic guns, not by soaking hits. Twin: no shields means point defence guns, armour, and the vital parts spread out.
