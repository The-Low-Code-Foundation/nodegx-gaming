CARRIER

A prototype: a 288 m carrier built like a warship, her crew living in two drums that spin round a weightless spine for their gravity. You come aboard as the most junior hand on her. Read the watch bill, muster with your watch, report to the officers, fight the drill fires, earn a turret, a fighter and the helm. Or roam free: ride a lift down into the drums, float along the spine, fly her on thrusters alone, and open the cutaway to see what is behind every wall.

There are no shields and no artificial anything you could not build: armour, point defence cannons against torpedoes, torpedo broadsides you fire by turning her side to the enemy, heat that has to be radiated away, and reaction mass that runs out. At action stations the ship sets condition one: suit up before she vents.
