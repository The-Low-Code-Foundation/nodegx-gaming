CARRIER

A prototype: a small warship, three or four crew on one weightless deck, with every station seconds away. Float from the flight deck to engineering, take the pilot's couch and fly her, man her turret, and open the cutaway to see what is behind every wall.

A prototype: a 314 m carrier built like a warship, her crew living in two drums that spin round a weightless spine for their gravity. She is home port: you serve on a cutter she carries in her hangar, as the cutter's fourth and newest hand. Wake in your bunk, report to the cutter's commander, learn her stations, ride a sortie, shoot under fire control's orders, qualify at the helm, and stand your watch back aboard. Or roam free: ride a lift down into the drums, float along the spine, fly her on thrusters alone, and open the cutaway to see what is behind every wall.

There are no shields and no artificial anything you could not build: armour, point defence cannons against torpedoes, torpedo broadsides and railguns you fire by turning her side to the enemy, heat that has to be radiated away, and reaction mass that runs out. At action stations the ship sets condition one: suit up before she vents.
