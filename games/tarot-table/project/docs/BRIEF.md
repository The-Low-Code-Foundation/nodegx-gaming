# Brief

## What this app is

A phone companion for a real Rider-Waite-style tarot deck: choose a spread, tap in each card you draw (suit, then number, upright or reversed), and get a plain, warm reading of that card in that position plus a question to ask the person. Tarot as a conversation starter, not a prophecy.

## Who uses it

A group around a table with a physical deck and nobody who knows tarot. One person holds the phone and reads aloud.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Predicting anything: the app never says what will happen, only what to talk about
- Saving a history of readings (ruled out by the owner)
- Card artwork: cards are typographic, so no images of the Rider-Waite deck are shipped
- Accounts, a backend, AI calls

_Agreed in the initial scoping conversation; the full record, including what was considered and rejected, is in `docs/decisions/000-initial-scope.md`._
