Which licence covers what in this folder

This folder is an app built with NodeGX. Its pages, data and assets are the app's own and belong to whoever
built it. NodeGX puts no licence on them.

The NodeGX runtime runs the app: noodl-app.png, load_terminator.js, noodl.deploy.js, serve.js. It is under the MIT licence printed below.
index-040c287995568811.js and index.html start the runtime, with the app filled in.
react.production.min.js and react-dom.production.min.js are React, under React's own MIT licence, stated at the top of each file.
noodl.deploy.js.LICENSE.txt lists the other open-source code inside noodl.deploy.js, each under its own licence.

The NodeGX editor and tools are under the GPL-3.0. What an app runs on is under the MIT licence.
